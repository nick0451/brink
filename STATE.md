- **Step 6 delivered (2026-10-04):**
  - **Force pools.**
    - `Forces {land, naval, air, readiness, mobilization}`. Each pool has strength and quality.
    - Starting `military` is split by the data field `force_mix` (default 60/20/20).
    - New production enters at the country's `military_tech` (scenario field, default 5 = 1.0× quality), so quality is a running weighted average.
  - **Readiness.**
    - Heads toward mobilization cap × funding. Funding = (production + arms received) ÷ upkeep, capped at 1.
    - A force whose spending no longer covers upkeep goes **hollow before it shrinks**. Measured: a cut to 2% military budget leaves 79% of strength but 52% of power after 6 turns.
    - `power()` = Σ strength × quality/5 × (0.5 + readiness). A fully funded peacetime force has power = strength, so the old calibration holds.
  - **Security driver.**
    - `domestic::security_breakdown`: Security = 10 + 65 × D/(D+H), where D = own power + Σ defenders' power × 0.5 × my Credibility(Back) reading of them.
    - H = Σ others' power × hostility (max of tension/100 and their −opinion/100) × `reach()`. `reach()` is 1.0 until regions exist.
    - Readings: 75 with no hostile power; NEU in the tense fixture 16.6; a guarantee from MAJ lifts it to 29.2.
  - **AI.**
    - `inputs::defence(view)` = own power + allies/guarantors × 0.5 × credibility_back. `threat()` uses it.
    - `ForeignView.military` is now an estimate of power, plus per-pool `forces` estimates.
  - **Arms transfers.**
    - `Order::ArmsTransfer` (one-off), `StartArmsStream` / `StopArmsStream`, `StreamKind::{Aid, Arms}`. `Stream::value()` gives the money equivalent (strength ÷ 0.5) so aid and arms compare.
    - Arms move from the supplier's pools pro rata, capped at 10% of supplier strength per turn. Incoming quality is capped at recipient tech + 2.
    - The recipient's `arms_origin` tracks the supplier's strength still in service (wears at upkeep). The supplier records `arms_out` / `arms_out_total` for step 8's defence burden.
    - Cutting an arms stream to a client in crisis is an Abandoned Back entry, exactly like aid.
  - **Gate 4 test (`step6.rs`).** MAJ arms NEU (3/turn × 16 turns), then cuts the supply, sanctions and denounces it; RIV then sanctions MAJ.

    - NEU's threat reading of MAJ is 14.0 vs 16.3 and its Security is higher.

- **Step 7 delivered (2026-10-04):**
  - **War model.**
    - `War {aim, attacker, defender, belligerents (side, band, allocation, losses), fronts, peace_offers}`. Each country's allocation is normalised across its wars.
    - Combat per turn: ratio = attack × (1 ± 0.25 seeded) ÷ (defence × 1.5).
    - Progress += 12 × ln(ratio) (×1.5 for Major), capped at ±25 per turn.
    - Losses are 3% × intensity × ratio^∓½ of committed strength. Losses wear `arms_origin` proportionally.
    - Weariness = casualty share × government sensitivity (democracy 1.5) + 0.5/turn; halved for the defender at home.
    - Enemies trade nothing (no rerouting). Tension baseline at war is 90; hostility is 1.0.
  - **Peace.**
    - Triggers: ±100 is decisive; both leaders' offers lead to a negotiated peace (|progress| > 25 wins); a leader at weariness ≥ 60 forces peace.
    - The winner gains +5 Legitimacy and the loser loses 10. Limited wins pay reparations of half a turn's revenue.
    - A Major win changes the loser's regime: alignment flips, Legitimacy drops to 30. For the player that is game over.
    - A punitive strike ends if the target offers peace within 2 turns, otherwise it becomes a Limited war.
  - **Ledger effects.**
    - A declaration writes a Coercion entry, triggers the attack shadows and an Aggression norm violation, and opens 2-turn Back tests for the defender's allies.
    - A defender-side joiner creates the Aggression norm claim.
    - Exits grade Abandoned if the side is losing, otherwise Partial, at the band's stake and the involvement's visibility. Peace grades remaining involvements Honoured.
    - Breaking a NAP writes an Abandoned entry (weight 1.5). War without a casus belli costs −15 Legitimacy and a −25 world reaction.
  - **Mobilization.**
    - `SetMobilization` levels raise the readiness cap (50 / 75 / 100 / 110%).
    - Growth drag is 0.6 / 1.9 / 3.75% per quarter. Full/Total at peace drains 1 Legitimacy per turn.
  - **AI.**
    - Band utility per DESIGN §14.9. Crisis responses are decided at once, then re-scored on assessments with 5 points of hysteresis.
    - The record value is the margin over the best alternative, so a chosen record is always positive and its lines still sum.
    - RepDelta scales with the guarantor's other commitments.
    - Leaders: `continue_war` is forward-only, with a "backing down so soon" term (20, fading over 4 turns).
    - Declaring war needs hostility; status quo −25, world reaction −10, no casus belli −30, attacking a great power −40 × (1 − risk).

- **v0.2 P10 delivered (2026-10-04):**
  - **Engine** (`sim-core/src/programme.rs`).

    - **Start:** security need × (1 − nuclear cover ÷ 3), "they have the bomb", cost, "a programme we can barely sustain" (by capability), exposure risk, non-proliferation pressure, a patron's displeasure, status quo. Minors never start one.

    - **Disclose & dismantle:** only small arsenals (≤ 1) or programmes. Weighed against the threat **as it would be without the arsenal** (the first version was circular and made Israel disarm in 1981).
