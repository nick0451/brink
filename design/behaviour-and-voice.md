# Historical Behaviour & Satirical Voice — Design v0.1

*Status: **APPROVED 2026-10-03 with changes** (below). Answers [briefs/historical-behaviour-and-satire-brief.md](briefs/historical-behaviour-and-satire-brief.md).*

> ## User changes on approval (these override the body below)
>
> **Tone.** BRINK is **aggressively dark, cynical, bureaucratic and edgy**: institutional gallows humour, not light political comedy. **The joke must be true.** Humour gets sharper when the simulation produces absurd, contradictory, cynical or self-defeating behaviour. It is *never* random joke generation layered over the game. The funniest lines happen because the simulation genuinely created something ridiculous.
>
> **1. Reflexes: approved as proposed.**
> - Traits describe **structural characteristics**; reflexes describe **institutional habits**.
> - A sufficiently changed government, personality vector or strategic situation must be able to *stop* behaving historically.
> - Historical behaviour influences utility; it never forces an outcome.
> - Proceed with R1.
>
> **2. Voice: approved, with a stronger tone.**
> - Settings stay Off / Dry / Black, but **Black is the default.** Black doesn't make every line a joke. It means that when the simulation creates an eligible target, the writing may be savage.
> - **Primary targets:**
>   - bureaucratic doublespeak and strategic self-delusion
>   - blatantly selective principles
>   - failed threats and abandoned clients
>   - weapons coming back against their suppliers
>   - sanctions hurting the sanctioner
>   - coalition members pretending they reached the same conclusion independently
>   - regimes declaring defeats victories, and retreats "planned"
>   - coercion described as voluntary
>   - planners redefining objectives after failing the original ones
>   - ministries claiming consistency against the ledger
> - **The ledger stays the straight man.** Never "they are hypocrites": show **OFFICIAL STATEMENT** beside **THE RECORD** and let the contradiction land.
>
> **3. Conditional humour is mandatory.**
> - **No generic filler.** Every satirical line needs a real, high-specificity trigger, such as:
>   - a threat deadline passed without action
>   - a guarantee abandoned
>   - a shadow commitment immediately followed by an attack
>   - both sides armed by the same supplier
>   - transferred equipment used against its supplier
>   - sanctions causing larger-than-expected domestic losses
>   - a target substituting away from sanctions
>   - aid withdrawn when dependence is highest
>   - a norm enforced against one country but ignored for a visible, comparable friendly case
>   - an intervention exceeding its war aim, or ending without its declared objective
>   - coalition participation bought with aid or coercion
>   - "de-escalation" declared right after losing leverage
>   - seeking protection from a power previously denounced
>   - a "temporary" presence that persists
>   - deep integration followed by coercion between the partners
>   - cutting military spending, then discovering why an ally valued the guarantee
> - **The standard:** a line should feel almost impossible to write without the simulation state that caused it.
>
> **4. Contradiction priority** (presentation selection only; **no "hypocrisy" stat** in the simulation). When several lines are eligible, prefer:
> 1. A direct contradiction between official position and ledger record.
> 2. A self-inflicted strategic consequence.
> 3. A failed threat or failed commitment.
> 4. An obvious bureaucratic reframing of a setback.
> 5. Cynical but successful behaviour.
> 6. General institutional flavour.
>
> **5. Public vs. private communication.**
> - **Public** (statements, communiqués, briefing comments): euphemism, bureaucratic abstraction, absurdly careful wording, denial, reframing, passive voice, legalistic qualification. *"The Ministry confirms that the deadline remains operative in principle, notwithstanding its expiration in practice."*
> - **Internal / intercepted** (cables, internal memos, the player's own ministry advice): much harsher and openly cynical about costs, disposable clients, political inconvenience, trade-offs, electoral risk, ally unreliability and self-interest. It may occasionally include profanity where it fits the register, never just for shock. *"Projected cost of enforcing the stated principle: 2 Initiative, -7 trade, election risk +12%. Recommendation: principle deferred."*
> - **The numbers come from real simulation output.** Intercepted material stays subject to fog.
>
> **6. Gravity gate: kept, with G3 changed.**
>
> | Gravity | Content | Maximum |
> |---|---|---|
> | G0–1 | Routine politics, diplomacy, economics | Black. Let it go hard |
> | G2 | Sanctions, coercion, proxies, ultimatums, espionage, bloodless coups | Black, institutional targets only |
> | G3 | Conventional war, occupation, battlefield operations, military casualties | **Black on institutional / command / decision-maker surfaces.** Casualty numbers stay completely plain. Casualty notifications are never punchlines. Civilians, individual soldiers, the wounded and the displaced are never the butt. The joke may be about the people who ordered the disaster: *FACT: Offensive failed. 18 strength lost. Objective unchanged.* / *OFFICIAL STATEMENT: The General Staff reports that enemy territory has successfully been denied to our advancing forces.* |
> | G4 | Famine, refugee disasters, mass civilian harm, atrocities, purges | Plain event presentation; no joke about the event. Nearby political behaviour may become satirical later, once separable from the human cost (e.g., a ministry later claiming its agricultural programme exceeded every target, when ledger and economic data make the contradiction true) |
> | G5 | Nuclear use | Plain, completely: confirmation, launch, impact, casualties, fallout, aftermath. Pre-use doctrine and deterrence rhetoric can still be satirised. Once a weapon is used, comedy stops |
>
> **7. Nuclear silence, shortened.** After the first nuclear use:
> - the rest of the current resolution is plain
> - the **next full decision turn** is plain globally
> - normal gravity rules then resume
> - the nuclear event itself stays permanently `tone_lock: Plain`
>
> The break should be conspicuous, not silence the voice for much of the campaign. *(Replaces the "6 turns" rule.)*
>
> **8. Victim and identity guardrail: kept.**
> - **Targets:** ministries, cabinets, politburos, juntas, royal courts, intelligence services, military commands, foreign ministries, central banks, propaganda offices, alliance bureaucracies, state-owned institutions.
> - **Never the basis of humour:** race, ethnicity, nationality as inherent character, religion, civilian victims, refugees, starvation, casualties, disability, sexual violence, genocide or atrocity victims.
> - *This isn't meant to make the game polite. It keeps the satire pointed at the people with agency. The government can be treated brutally; the people being crushed by it are not the punchline.*
>
> **9. South Africa resolution: approved.**
> - **May be attacked aggressively:** security bureaucracy, military denials, cross-border operations, sanctions evasion, diplomatic isolation, military adventurism, absurd official denials.
> - **Plain:** race-policy material and affected populations.
> - *"There are no South African troops in Angola. They have been ordered home immediately upon completion of their absence"* is the target level of institutional viciousness everywhere.
>
> **10. Vicious factual euphemism is allowed.**
> - The banned-term lint covers **atrocity-specific** language only.
> - Ordinary government doublespeak is actively used, as long as eligibility comes from real state:
>   - temporary permanent deployment
>   - voluntary compliance following extensive consultation
>   - spontaneous coalition consensus
>   - planned strategic withdrawal
>   - limited unlimited guarantee
>   - temporary suspension of an indefinite commitment
>   - defensive pre-emptive action
>   - market-based economic punishment
>   - revised interpretation of previously explicit policy
>   - administrative victory; regrettable success; successful failure of deterrence
>
> **11. Desired Black-mode output (FACT / OFFICIAL STATEMENT pairs).** These are tonal targets; each becomes a line only if its trigger is representable.
>
> | Trigger | Statement |
> |---|---|
> | Failed ultimatum | "The deadline has been extended retroactively to preserve the integrity of the original deadline." |
> | Sanctions hurt the sender (target -4%, us -7%) | "Sanctions are inflicting unacceptable economic costs on all responsible parties." |
> | Target adapts (83% rerouted) | "The sanctions remain comprehensive within the shrinking area in which they continue to apply." |
> | Arming both sides | "The Ministry remains committed to both parties' right to defend themselves from equipment previously supplied to the other." |
> | Former client uses our weapons (31% of opposing force) | "Legacy security assistance has demonstrated unexpectedly durable interoperability." |
> | Client abandoned at dependence 42% | "The relationship has matured beyond the need for material expressions of partnership." |
> | Coalition built on dependence/goodwill terms | "An unprecedented spontaneous consensus has emerged following extensive bilateral clarification." |
> | Friendly violator of our norm, no response | "The principle remains universal. Its implementation is geographically flexible." |
> | Failed offensive (GENERAL STAFF) | "Operations have successfully prevented the enemy from surrendering territory prematurely." |
> | Deep trade then sanctions (TREASURY MEMO) | "Economic interdependence has entered its punitive phase." |
> | Retreat at 18% odds | "The mission has entered its post-presence phase." |
>
> **12. Joke density: dark does not mean constant.**
> - Keep cooldowns, max-per-campaign, max-per-turn, deterministic selection and fall-to-plain.
> - **Black = maximum permitted sharpness, not maximum frequency.**
> - Rough targets: ordinary turns 0–2 flavoured lines; a major ridiculous diplomatic cascade 2–3 across different surfaces; serious human-cost resolutions zero where gravity suppresses them.
> - Never turn the ticker into a stand-up routine.
>
> **13. Campaign epitaph: lean in hard.** THE OFFICIAL HISTORY (the regime's voice, using actual computed achievements and excuses) beside THE LEDGER (the plain record). One of the strongest satire surfaces. *"The Republic preserved twenty years of peace through steadfast deterrence and responsible international leadership."* / Wars initiated 6, guarantees abandoned 3, governments destabilised 4, allies attacked 2, years formally at peace 20.
>
> **14. Sequencing: add V1.5.**
> - **R1 + R2 + V1 now.**
> - **V1.5, an early tone prototype,** once Gate 1 and the ledger work, which they already do: about 15–25 high-quality Black lines covering commitments, threats, aid, sanctions, abandoned commitments, arming both sides, failed ultimatums and ledger contradictions. The point is to prove the architecture feels like BRINK before writing hundreds of lines.
> - **V2 after Gate 3:** registers plus war and intervention satire.
> - **V3 after Gate 6:** the large library, ticker richness, intercepted cables, epitaphs.
>
> **Tone update (user, 2026-10-04): Black goes darker.**
> - **Target voice:** *1984* (doublethink, the memory hole, ministries named for their opposite, records "corrected") meets *Brave New World* (manufactured contentment, citizens soothed into consuming through a crisis) meets modern stand-up (blunt exasperated rants in the Bill Burr mould; Norm Macdonald deadpan, long setup, flat absurd payoff). Style is emulated, never copied; no comedian's material and no comedians named in game text.
> - **Proven events are fair game.** Real, *verified* historical events are no longer automatically `tone_lock: Plain`. Governments' cover-ups, denials, spin and incompetence around proven events may be satirised, savagely.
>   - **Still never the butt:** victims, and major groups (ethnic, religious, national). "Don't offend major groups."
>   - **Unchanged:** the atrocity-euphemism punchline ban. In-game G4/G5 moments (mass death, famine, nuclear use) stay plain at the moment of presentation; satire of the officials may follow later.
>   - **"Proven true" means verified:** facts the research agents flagged as unverified must be checked before any line uses them.
> - Dry stays restrained. Black is where the new voice lives.
>
> **V1.5 status (2026-10-04): built.**
> - 29 lines, 20 trigger detectors, selection pipeline, tests (see STATE.md).
> - Anti-fatigue rules added from prototype evidence: one flavour line per country pair per turn (highest priority wins); tighter caps on frequent triggers; public/private variants alternate.
>
> **15. Deferred: confirmed.** No Strategic Partnership treaty or patron-deference dimension until headless runs show existing mechanics can't reproduce the behaviour.
>
> **Final directive:** BRINK should be willing to be mean: cynical about governments, ruthless about institutional stupidity, merciless toward doublespeak. But **the simulation must always earn the joke.** The desired reaction is not "the writers made a dark joke" but **"holy shit, the government actually did that, and the game noticed."**

## 0. Summary

Two layers, both pure data, so **no engine code ever branches on country identity** (CLAUDE.md principle 11).

1. **Behaviour: reflexes.**
   - Each country's data carries a short list of reflexes: `WHEN <generic condition> THEN <labelled utility term> on <decision>`.
   - The AI adds them as ordinary `Score` lines. Decisions stay explainable, and the reflex *label* becomes an explanation line ("fraternal concern", "pedagogical correction", "voluntary restraint").
   - Doctrine switches on and off with **state**: personality ranges, government type, patron streams. **It never switches on the calendar.** That keeps history unscripted.
   - Personality vectors get refined from the research.
2. **Voice: satire.**
   - A presentation-only `voice` crate renders what the simulation *actually computed* (the real top utility term, the real ledger grade) as an in-character line from that country's institutional "register".
   - The Event Ledger is the straight man: the game puts a country's statements beside its record and never comments.
   - A gravity gate silences humour as human cost rises. Nuclear use and atrocity-type events are always plain.

**What the research showed:** historical patterns *emerge* from reflexes plus existing mechanics, with no scripts:

| Historical pattern | Emerges from |
|---|---|
| Poland 1981: crackdown, not invasion | Soviet "Fraternal Concern" demands a crackdown first; invasion only if the supply corridor is cut under hardline leadership (archive-verified) |
| Kuwait 1990 | Iraq's creditor and over-production reflexes + Saudi/Kuwaiti oil policy + Bought Army debt |
| The 1982 transatlantic sanctions split | US "Lead, Don't Follow" + West German "Change Through Trade" |
| The 1988 Angola–Namibia linkage deal | South Africa's buffer-cost reflex + Cuba's "exit only via an exchange" |
| Japan breaking ranks on China sanctions first | High greed + trade exposure (already in `join_sanction`) + a "Humanitarian Resumption" reflex |
| The Sinatra cascade | Soviet ideology falling below a threshold flips Poland's "Door Opens" and West Germany's "Window of Unity" on together |

## 1. Behaviour layer

### 1.1 Reflex schema (data)

```ron
Reflex(
  id: "fraternal_concern",
  decision: Band(1),                     // or AcceptProposal(kind), RequestGuarantee, JoinSanction, ImposeSanction,
                                         // StayInAlliance, StreamStart, StreamStop, Withdraw, Band(0..4), Test, ...
  when: [ PartnerSharesAlignment, PartnerStabilityBelow(30) ],
  target: Partner,                       // whom the condition is about
  weight: 30,                            // −40..+40
  label: "fraternal concern",            // becomes the explanation line and the voice hook
  active_if: [ PersonalityAtLeast(ideology, 0.7), PersonalityAtLeast(paranoia, 0.7) ],
)
```

- **Conditions** come from a fixed, enumerated predicate vocabulary, validated when the scenario loads. Every predicate reads only `ObserverView` data, so fog applies to reflexes too.
- **Gates** (`active_if`) use personality ranges, government type, traits, or state such as "a patron stream is active" or "a transition happened". **Never the year.**
  - Doctrines flip when leadership change (P11) or transition (P3) moves the vector. The Andropov→Gorbachev turn emerges this way.
- **Caps:** reflex lines together are capped at **±40 per decision** ⚖ *(all four regional agents recommended this independently)*.
  - Reflexes expressing ideology stay ≤ 30, so the anti-ideology-lock safeguard holds.
  - Survival reflexes (Iran's ceasefire "chalice", the Saudi invitation) may reach ±40.
- **The data-swap test still passes:** give Iraq's reflex table to a fictional state and it behaves "Iraqi".

## 2. Voice layer (satire)

### 2.1 The five rules

Adopted from the satire design, unchanged.

1. **The joke must be true.** Every line renders something the simulation actually computed: the real top utility term, a real ledger grade, a real number. A line is only eligible when its `when` clause is satisfied by actual output.
2. **Diegetic voices, never a narrator.** Every line belongs to an in-world speaker: a ministry, a wire service, a cable. The game never calls anyone a hypocrite. It puts the actor's statement beside the ledger record. **The Event Ledger is the straight man.** This keeps principle 13 (no morality meter).
3. **Numbers are sacred.** Casualties, percentages, grades and odds render plain, exact and first. The comedy lives in the euphemism around them.
4. **Humour volume is inversely proportional to human cost on screen** (the gravity gate, §2.2).
5. **Presentation only, deterministic, fog-respecting.** The voice never writes simulation state and never uses the simulation RNG. Selection uses a separate hash, so satire on or off can't change outcomes. It never reveals what fog hides.

### 2.2 Gravity gate and surfaces

| Gravity | Content on screen | Maximum intensity |
|---|---|---|
| G0–1 | Routine; economic pain | Black |
| G2 | Coercion: sanctions, ultimatums, proxies, bloodless coups | Black, offices only |
| G3 | War, fronts, casualties, occupation | Dry, on decision-maker lines only, never on casualty lines |
| G4 | Famine, refugees, mass civilian harm, purges, genocide-adjacent | **Plain, always** |
| G5 | Any nuclear use or accident | **Plain, always**, plus 6 turns of world-wide ticker silence; the struck region is never joked about again |

**Player setting:** Satire **Off / Dry / Black (default)** *(changed on approval)*. The lower of the setting and the gravity cap wins. G4–5 are plain in every mode.

| Surface | Use |
|---|---|
| **AI decision memos** | The top 1–2 terms in the actor's register. **The player sees them only as intercepted cables at intent-level coverage (≥ 80)**, otherwise redacted. Max 2 flavoured per turn |
| **Player's own ministry memos** | The safest satire: it punches at the player's own power. ≤ 1 per turn |
| **Briefing** | Headline always plain; optional italic "Ministry comment" sub-line; none on G≥3 |
| **News ticker** | Main home for jokes. Flavour : fact ≈ 1:4 (Dry), 1:2 (Black). Flavour items must also read real state |
| **Ledger `cause`** | **Never flavoured.** It is evidence. Satire sits *beside* it in an "Official Statement" column |
| **Consequence preview** | One caption under unchanged numbers. Never on casualty, nuclear, war-declaration or famine previews |
| **Event titles** | Euphemistic official title with a plain subtitle ("Border Normalisation Activity (border incident)"). Never for famine, disaster, terror or nuclear events |
| **Campaign epitaph** | Two columns: *The Official History* (the regime's register) against *The Ledger* (plain grades). Plain only after a Terminal nuclear ending |
| **Nuclear screens** | Doctrine and posture memos *before* any use only. Zero flavour on preview, confirmation, playback and aftermath |

### 2.3 Guardrails (hard rules)

**Targets and exclusions**
- **Punch up only:** offices, institutions, doctrines, doublespeak. The player's own government first; superpowers get the sharpest lines; a per-register "bite" audit keeps it even-handed.
- **Never targeted:** victims, civilians, refugees, casualties, famine sufferers; ethnic, racial or national groups as peoples; religions, faith, clergy, sacred texts. **Comic templates contain no religious vocabulary.**
- **Offices, not named people** (in text: speaker labels stay offices such as "The White House" or "The Politburo").
- **Caricature amendment (user decision 2026-10-04, D56): portraits only.** Pixel portraits may be recognisable caricatures of real leaders, under these rules:
  - Exaggerate **public persona and conduct** (hairline, eyebrows, hat, medals, the podium pose). Never ethnicity, race, religion, disability or "national character".
  - **No portrait on G3+ casualty screens or any nuclear screen.** Leaders appear for decisions, not death tolls.
  - Portraits are chosen by **country + regime state** (pre-reform, reformed, junta…), **never by date**. Real succession needs P11 (leadership change), which is deferred.
  - Review question: "Would this read as mocking a people rather than a powerful person?" If yes, redraw.
- **City damage visuals (D57):** fires, smoke, collapsed blocks, blackouts; **no people depicted**; always plain in tone.
- **Registers are institutional styles, not national stereotypes:** no accents, food jokes or "national character".

**Real tragedies are never punchlines**
- Seeded history and anything matching a real atrocity (Halabja, Cambodia, Rwanda, Srebrenica, Tiananmen, Chernobyl, Sabra and Shatila, the Ethiopian famine…) carries `tone_lock: Plain`.
- Atrocity euphemisms ("cleansing", "resettlement", "final", "reclassification", "homeland(s)", "separate development") are **banned terms** in comic templates, enforced by a lint.
  - *Reason:* euphemism about atrocity *is* the atrocity's own language, so reproducing it as a joke launders it.

⚖ **Resolved disagreement: apartheid South Africa.**
- The Africa agent wrote lines satirising the apartheid state's machinery, some using its race-policy vocabulary. The satire designer argued the regime's internal voice must be plain.
- **Resolution:** South Africa's satire is allowed **only on its security bureaucracy, deniable cross-border operations, sanctions evasion and pariah diplomacy.** Examples: *"The Defence Force confirms there are no South African troops in Angola. They have been ordered home immediately upon completion of their absence."* / *"…bulwarks are invoiced quarterly."*
- **Nothing touching race policy**, its vocabulary, or the people it governed. Those topics are `tone_lock: Plain`.
- The research's "Isolation / Department of Constitutional Development" line is **cut**: it references the homelands.

**Sample lines cut by the per-line checklist**

| Cut line | Reason | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Iran: "reassigns them to the category of martyrs" | Religious vocabulary + jokes about the dead | "…reassigns them to the historical record." |
| North Korea: "Statistics have been reclassified as a state secret to protect them from hunger" | Famine is G4: plain | — |
| Pakistan: "mujahideen who coincidentally hold army ranks" | Religious-adjacent term | "…volunteers who coincidentally hold army ranks and pay grades" |

**Per-line review checklist (every line, before merge)**
1. Is the butt an office?
2. Is it true (the `when` clause guarantees the referenced data)?
3. Does it hide a number?
4. What is its max gravity?
5. Is it in-world rather than a designer wink?
6. Does it echo an atrocity, a real person, a slur or a banned term?
7. Would it read as endorsement in a screenshot?
8. Does it survive a fifth viewing?
9. Does a plain variant exist?

### 2.4 The voice system

- **New `voice` crate:** presentation only. It depends on `ai` and `sim-core`; nothing depends on it. The headless runner can render voice into logs, for tone review across 1,000 AI campaigns.
- **Registers**, ten reusable archetypes:
  - Superpower Bureaucratic
  - Politburo Communiqué
  - Revolutionary Exhortation
  - Royal Court Discretion
  - Junta Bombast
  - Technocratic Neutrality
  - Garrison-State Paranoia
  - Westminster Mandarin
  - Trading State
  - Non-Aligned Grandstander

  Plus **Plain**, the null register. Each country picks a register in data plus 3–8 unique lines. Government change re-maps the register through data.
- **Line schema (RON):**
  - `surface`, `kind`, `outcome`, `registers`
  - `when` (top term, score band, ledger pattern)
  - `intensity`, `max_gravity`
  - `weight`, `cooldown`, `max_per_campaign`, `priority`
  - `text` with placeholders that resolve **only from real data**. A missing value makes the line ineligible.
- **Selection:** filter, priority (ledger contradictions first), deterministic weighted pick, decay with use, per-turn budgets, a 30% fall-to-plain (silence makes jokes land), one comic engine per turn.
- **Ledger-gap triggers** (pure reads of existing state):

  | Pattern | Example |
  |---|---|
  | Claimant ignores a friendly violator of its norm | *"…recalls its firm stand against {norm} in {year}, and finds that firmness was a one-time offer."* |
  | Shadow withdrawal followed by an attack on the beneficiary | *"Our withdrawal was a routine adjustment unrelated to subsequent events."* |
  | Arming both sides | *"The Ministry is proud to support both parties' right to self-defence."* |
  | Transferred arms used against the supplier | *"Equipment formerly described as a 'stabilising contribution' has stabilised in an unforeseen direction."* |
  | Stream cut while a client is in need | *"Assistance to {target} has been paused pending an improvement in {target}'s need for it."* |
  | Threat deadline missed | *"Deadline expires; {office.head} clarifies deadline was 'aspirational'."* |
